This is a polite rate update, not a contract change.

Thanks,
[Your name]

How to write a freelance rate increase email

Keep it short. Clients need three facts: the new rate, when it starts, and what stays the same. Then stop.

  1. Put the relationship or project in the subject so they do not have to hunt.
  2. State current rate and new rate in one sentence. Skip a long apology.
  3. Name the effective date. Without it, the raise feels optional.
  4. Say what does not change: response times, deliverable types, revision rounds.

What this is not

This is a rate update note from you. It is not a contract rewrite. It is not legal advice. It is not a threat.

  • Do not invent penalties or ultimatums. You are announcing a rate for ongoing work.
  • Do not over-justify. A short reason is enough. A manifesto invites debate.
  • Do not apologize for raising rates. Costs and demand change. That is normal.

Why freelancers stall on this email

Independent designers, developers, and consultants dread the raise note. Generic blogs are long and awkward. A clear email plus a one-page PDF they can file is enough for most retainers and repeat clients.
